# Version 1.0.4 - Performance Improvements & Better Experience

**Released:** December 7, 2025

We've made PR Tracker faster and more responsive! This update focuses on improving performance across the app and streamlining your experience.

---

## What's New

### Faster Loading Times

We've optimized how data loads throughout the app, making everything feel snappier and more responsive.

**Past Workouts Page**

- Loads your workout history faster than before
- Smoother scrolling through your past workouts
- Quicker access to your complete workout records

**Charts Page**

- Exercise progression charts load more quickly
- Faster navigation between different exercises
- Improved responsiveness when viewing your progress

**Weekly Summary**

- Updates instantly when you view your dashboard
- Shows only this week's workouts for a clearer, more focused view
- Faster loading with optimized data fetching

### Cleaner Interface

We've simplified the interface to make it easier to focus on what matters most.

**Workout Confirmation**

- Removed unnecessary workout count display
- Cleaner confirmation screen after completing workouts
- More streamlined experience

**Weekly Summary Card**

- Simplified display focusing on this week's progress
- Cleaner, less cluttered view
- Easier to see your current week's achievements at a glance

---

## Improvements

### Better Performance

The app now feels more responsive overall. Whether you're navigating between pages, viewing your workout history, or checking your progress charts, everything should feel faster and smoother.

### Focused Weekly View

Your weekly summary now shows only this week's workouts, giving you a clearer picture of your current progress without unnecessary information.
